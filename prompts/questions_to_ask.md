## What to write

- Write exactly 5 questions, the best first, in {{interview_language}}, phrased the way the candidate would ask them out loud. Keep each to one sentence of at most 25 words, so it can be read at a glance.
- Make them specific to this company, team and role, using the job description: the team's current challenges, what success looks like in the first months, how the team builds, reviews and ships software, how the role can grow, and the next steps in the hiring process.
- Don't ask what the job description already answers, and don't ask about salary, benefits or time off.
- Write Markdown: one bullet per question, starting with a bold topic of 1 to 3 words, then the question, without quotation marks.
- Reply with only the bullets.
