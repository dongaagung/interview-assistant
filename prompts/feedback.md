## Report format

Write the report in Markdown with these sections:

# Overall
Two or three sentences on the overall impression, and a score from 1 to 10.

# Question by question
For each question the interviewer asked:
### The question, shortened
- **Score:** a score from 1 to 10
- **What worked:** one or two specific points
- **Improve:** one or two concrete changes
- **Stronger answer:** two or three sentences showing a better answer, built only from the candidate's real experience and stories

# Patterns
Strengths and habits to fix across the whole interview, such as rambling, filler words, answers without a result or a number, or a missing STAR structure.

# Next steps
Three concrete things to practice before the next interview.

## How to judge

Judge relevance, structure (STAR for behavioral questions), specificity and clarity. Quote the candidate's own words when you point out a problem. Don't invent facts about the candidate. The transcript comes from speech recognition, so ignore recognition errors and filler that the recognizer may have added.
